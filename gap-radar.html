What is prediction market arbitrage?

Arbitrage means buying the same thing in two places at different prices. In prediction markets, it usually means buying Yes on one platform and No on another when the two prices add up to less than $1. In practice, fees, timing, rule differences and how much is actually available at that price often wipe out the gap. Gap Radar shows you the numbers. It doesn't place trades or tell you what to do.
