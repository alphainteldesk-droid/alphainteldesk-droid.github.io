Rule Risk

Same question, different rules. Two sites can list the same event and still settle it differently: another deadline, another source, another rule if it's cancelled. A price gap between them isn't a gap if the contracts aren't the same. Here is every market we match, and what its rules agree and disagree on.

How this page works

What do the colours mean?Red: we found a real difference in the rules. Amber: something we couldn't confirm, so read the rules yourself. Green: the rules line up on everything we check.
What gets checked?End dates, thresholds, who decides the answer (the resolution source), what happens if the event is cancelled, tie and overtime rules, and whether the two sites' prices behave like the same contract.
What is "Identical rules"?Both sites publish the same rules text, word for word. Alpha Arcade often does this with Polymarket's markets.
How does this connect to the Gap Scanner?Red pairs never appear as a price gap. Amber pairs can appear, graded B. Green pairs are graded A.
