The same question can trade at different prices on different prediction markets. How to read it: green is the lowest price for that outcome, red the highest. The gap is the difference in cents per $1 share, minus each site's trading fee, shown site by site under every gap (how it works). Prices are midpoints, so the bid/ask spread can shrink a gap further. Vol is the total traded on each site's market, 24h the last day (Polymarket and Alpha Arcade in dollars, Kalshi in contracts). Low volume can mean a price is hard to trade at. Matches are automatic. Always check each site's rules before you trade.

The details

Fees

All three sites charge the same kind of fee when your order fills against one already on the book (a "taker" order). It's biggest at 50¢ and shrinks toward 1¢ and 99¢. We subtract both sites' fees from every gap:

fee per $1 share = rate × price × (1 − price)

Not included yet: the bid/ask spread (we use midpoints), how much you can trade at that price, Kalshi's per-order rounding on 1–2 contract orders, deposit costs, and money tied up until settlement. Limit orders that wait on the book usually pay no fee. Fee rates last checked against each site's official page: Nov 10, 2026 (checked weekly) · Kalshi · Polymarket · Alpha Arcade

Fair price

fair ≈ (price₁ × $traded₁ + price₂ × $traded₂ + …) ÷ total $traded

Kalshi volume (contracts) is converted to dollars at its price. It's built only from these sites' prices, not a forecast. A site far from it may just have a thin order book.

Fine-print grades

FINE PRINT AEvery check passed, and both sites name the same resolution source.
FINE PRINT BEvery check we can run passed, but one site doesn't name its source, they use different news outlets, or only one says what happens if it's cancelled.
Not shownDifferent deadline, threshold, scope or resolution source. That isn't a real gap.

Matching and grades are automatic, from each site's published rules. Kalshi, Polymarket and Alpha Arcade side by side: other gap trackers skip Alpha Arcade.

What is prediction market arbitrage?

Buying the same outcome for less on one site than it costs on another, usually Yes on one and No on the other, so the pair pays $1 whatever happens. Fees, timing, rule differences and thin order books often wipe the gap out. Gap Scanner shows the numbers. It doesn't place trades or tell you what to do.
