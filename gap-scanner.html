How to read it: green is the lowest price for that outcome, red the highest. The gap is the difference in cents per $1 share, minus each site's estimated taker fee. Prices are midpoints, so the bid/ask spread can shrink a gap further. Vol is the total traded on each site's market, 24h the last day (Polymarket and Alpha Arcade in dollars, Kalshi in contracts). Low volume can mean a price is hard to trade at. Matches are automatic. Always check each site's rules before you trade.

How the scanner picks gaps

All three sites first, true matches only

Gaps priced on all three sites (Polymarket, Kalshi and Alpha Arcade) are listed first. Below them come gaps between two sites. Every market has to match: same event, same outcome, same end date and no rule warnings. "Similar" markets never count.

Real trading behind it

We skip markets with almost no trading, prices under 3¢ or over 97¢, markets about to close, and gaps so large they usually mean a stale or mismatched market.

Three markets, one screen

Kalshi, Polymarket and Alpha Arcade side by side. Other gap trackers skip Alpha Arcade. We don't.

After fees, not before

A 6¢ difference can shrink to 2¢, or to nothing, once each platform takes its fee. We do that math for you, using each site's published fee formula for that kind of market.

Rules checked

Two markets can look identical but settle differently, with a different deadline or data source. Those are left out, so a rules difference isn't mistaken for a real gap.

Checked every 20 minutes

Every gap shows when it was last checked. Prices move fast, and gaps can close at any time.

What is prediction market arbitrage?

Arbitrage means buying the same thing in two places at different prices. In prediction markets, it usually means buying Yes on one platform and No on another when the two prices add up to less than $1. In practice, fees, timing, rule differences and how much is actually available at that price often wipe out the gap. Gap Scanner shows you the numbers. It doesn't place trades or tell you what to do.
