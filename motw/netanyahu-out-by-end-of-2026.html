Market of the Week #1 · picked Nov 19, 2026

Netanyahu out by end of 2026?

Israel's coalition clock runs out before this market does. If his rival builds a government by Dec 20, YES wins. If talks stall, Netanyahu stays on as caretaker and NO wins.

The case for YES

  • His bloc won 53 of 120 seats on Oct 27, short of the 61 needed to govern.
  • President Herzog gave rival Gadi Eisenkot the first shot at forming a government, after 67 lawmakers backed him to Netanyahu's 46.
  • Anti-Netanyahu parties already hold a working majority in the new Knesset.
  • Eisenkot, Bennett, Golan and Liberman have all ruled out joining a government with Netanyahu.

The case for NO

  • Eisenkot's numbers likely need Arab-led parties' support, which parts of his own camp object to.
  • Liberman says he won't back a minority government, even a temporary one, which closes Eisenkot's easiest route.
  • Netanyahu doesn't have to win. If he stops Eisenkot, he stays in office as caretaker.
  • If Eisenkot misses Dec 20, the next candidate gets 28 days with no extension, which runs to about Jan 20, 2027, after this market closes.

YES pays $1 if Netanyahu announces he'll resign, steps down or is removed as Prime Minister by Dec 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET. An announcement alone counts, even if he leaves later.

NO pays $1 if he's still Prime Minister (including as caretaker) at that time.
