How Movers works

In plain English: a mover is a market where the crowd changed its mind fast: the price of an outcome went up or down by at least 5¢ in a day, and enough money changed hands that it's a real move, not one small trade.

What counts as a move

At least 5¢ up or down in 24 hours on one outcome, and the price is between 5¢ and 95¢ (near-certain outcomes are noisy). The market must end more than 3 days from now.

Real trading behind it

At least $25,000 traded in 24 hours on Polymarket, 15,000 contracts on Kalshi, or $100 on Alpha Arcade. Moves are ranked by size, weighted by how much was traded.

Left out on purpose

Sports, price-bracket ladders (for example "Bitcoin above $X") and "will someone say a word" markets. They swing all day without news.

Compared across all three sites

When the same market trades on another site, we show its price there too. If one site moved and the others didn't, that's worth a look. Check the Gap Scanner for gaps after fees.

"Why?" is a news search

The Why? button opens a Google News search for the market, so you can read what happened. It isn't our analysis.
