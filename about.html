Why Alpha Arcade?

Plenty of sites cover Polymarket and Kalshi. Almost nobody covers Alpha Arcade, the prediction market on Algorand. We cover all three side by side, so you can see the whole picture in one place.

Alpha Arcade is also built differently. Kalshi is a traditional, company-run exchange. Alpha Arcade runs on Algorand ($ALGO), a decentralized public blockchain, from the ground up: its markets and payouts are smart contracts that anyone can check.

Instant finality

Algorand settles a block in about 3 seconds, and once a trade is in, it's final. No waiting for confirmations and no chain splits.

Never gone down

Algorand has run without a network outage since its mainnet launched in June 2019.

Built for the quantum era

A leader in post-quantum security: Algorand has protected its history with quantum-resistant signatures since 2022, added quantum-resistant accounts in 2026, and is working toward full coverage by 2027.

Tiny, steady fees

Network fees are a fraction of a cent and don't spike when the network gets busy.
