Disclosures

Plain-English notes on what this site is, how it's funded, and what to keep in mind. Last updated November 2, 2026.

Not financial advice

Everything on Alpha Intel Desk is for information and entertainment only. Nothing here is financial, investment, legal or tax advice, and nothing is a recommendation to make any trade. Do your own research and make your own decisions.

Prediction markets involve risk

You can lose some or all of the money you put into a prediction market. Prices can move fast, and markets can resolve in ways that surprise people. Only trade what you can afford to lose.

Referral links

Some links on this site are referral links. If you sign up through one, we may receive a reward, at no extra cost to you. This helps keep the site free.

We may trade

The people behind this site may trade in markets we write about. We don't tell you which side to take, and our coverage is not a signal of our own positions.

Markets we create

From time to time we may create markets on Alpha Arcade, for example our Market of the Week. Market creators earn a share of trading fees, and we may provide liquidity in markets we create. We will say so on the page whenever a market is ours.

Our data

We use public market data from Polymarket, Kalshi and Alpha Arcade, and public on-chain records. Data can be delayed, incomplete or wrong. Always check prices and rules on the market's own site before trading. Some data, such as liquidity reward information, is shown with a delay.

Visitor analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages people read and roughly where they visit from. It uses cookies and doesn't tell us who you are. We don't sell visitor data. You can block it with your browser's privacy settings or an ad blocker, and the site works the same.

Alerts

Our alerts, including Fresh Wallet Alerts, flag unusual activity, not wrongdoing, and are not a prediction or financial advice. Wallet activity is public on-chain data. Kalshi does not show who trades, so Kalshi alerts show large trades only.

Not affiliated

Alpha Intel Desk is independent. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Polymarket, Kalshi or Alpha Arcade. Their names belong to their owners.

Where you live matters

Prediction markets are not available everywhere. Each platform sets its own rules about who can use it. It's up to you to check that a platform is legal and available where you live.
