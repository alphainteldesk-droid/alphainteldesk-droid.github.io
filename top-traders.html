How this page works

Where do the names come from?Each site's own public leaderboard. We don't rank anyone ourselves.
What is a "biggest move"?The largest single trade that trader made in the last 7 days, by dollars. It can be one leg of a bigger plan, so don't read it as a tip.
Why no moves for Kalshi traders?Kalshi keeps positions private unless a trader chooses to share them. Instead we show the largest single trades of the week in the busiest Kalshi markets we track. Those are anonymous.
Why is Alpha Arcade "all-time"?Alpha Arcade publishes one all-time board. Accounts without a username appear as a short wallet tag, the same way Alpha Arcade shows them.
How fresh is it?The boards are re-read about once an hour.
