How this page works

What sets off an alert?A wallet with almost no history makes a large trade on one side of a market that closes soon. Our model decides what counts as "new", "large" and "soon"; we don't publish the exact settings, so they can't be gamed.
Why does one alert list several wallets?After the first trade we wait a few minutes. Other new wallets that take the same side in that window are grouped into the same alert. Several new wallets moving together is a stronger signal than one.
How do you keep score?Every alert is followed until its market settles, then marked WON or LOST. Nothing is ever removed, so the record is honest.
Which sites?Polymarket today, because its trades and wallets are public. Kalshi trades are anonymous, so new wallets can't be seen there. Alpha Arcade is next.
Why no sports?Large trades on tonight's game are routine. We look at politics, economics, world events, crypto and culture.
